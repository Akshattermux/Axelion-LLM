Axelion is a custom decoder-only Transformer language model engineered from first principles by Axionik.
The architecture implements Rotary Position Embeddings (RoPE), RMSNorm for numerical stability, Grouped-Query Attention (GQA) for fast decoding, SwiGLU non-linear activations, KV Caching, and weight tying between the token embedding matrix and the language model head.
